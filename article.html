Real Madrid Step Up Interest in Signing Arsenal Midfielder Declan Rice

September 9, 2026 | Spotify KE

Real Madrid are reportedly keeping a close eye on Declan Rice as Arsenal work to secure the long-term future of one of their most important players.

The Arsenal midfielder has become central to Mikel Arteta's team since joining the club from West Ham in 2023.

Rice has continued to develop his influence in midfield, combining defensive intelligence with his ability to carry the ball forward and contribute in attacking areas.

Real Madrid make Rice a priority target

Reports indicate that senior figures at Real Madrid are strong admirers of Rice and could consider making him a priority signing if an opportunity becomes available.

Arsenal, however, are determined to keep the midfielder and are working on fresh contract terms.

Rice's current contract runs until 2028, but Arsenal want to secure his future as part of their efforts to keep the core of their squad together.

Arsenal want to keep Rice

Rice has given Arsenal little reason to believe that he is looking for an exit. He remains an important part of the club's plans and has established himself as one of the team's key midfielders.

Real Madrid's interest is understandable given Rice's age, experience and development into an all-round midfielder.

Arsenal will be hoping that a new contract can end any uncertainty surrounding his future.
