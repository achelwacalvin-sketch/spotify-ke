Kenya to Host Regional Football Legends Tournament

September 9, 2026 | Spotify KE

Kenya is set to host a regional football legends tournament, bringing together former football stars for an event focused on competition, football heritage and community engagement.

The tournament will feature former football players, including legends associated with Kenyan football.

The event will give former players an opportunity to return to the pitch while celebrating their contribution to football in Kenya and the wider region.

Celebrating football legends

The tournament is expected to keep former football stars connected to the sport while giving fans an opportunity to see some of the players who helped shape football in the region.
